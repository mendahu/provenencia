# Spike 13 — Completed

One short paragraph per landed PR, newest first. Written by the PR that lands it.

## S13-02 — Gender in the person header

**Landed 2026-11-01** in [#659](https://github.com/mendahu/provenencia/pull/659). Every person row or tile can be colored by gender from the one header type: `PersonHeader` carries the displayed gender and sex at birth terms (the rank-1 kept term, how many values are displayed, and the Property's label), on the wire as the new `TermFacts` and in Swift as `CatalogTermFacts`. A stored header keeps term ids and keys; term and Property labels are filled when it is read, so a relabel shows at once. `PersonGenderTreatment` reads only the header and the Person page moves onto it, with the badge, spoken label and thumb colors unchanged; the read from detail fields is deleted, and only the product `gender` and `sex_at_birth` Properties feed it. `EventHeader`'s type moved onto the same Go shape with no wire change. The Persons list, Promote and search carry the terms and do not draw them.

## S13-01 — Person claims read

**Landed 2026-10-31** in [#655](https://github.com/mendahu/provenencia/pull/655). The engine answers which records make up a person: `ListPersonClaims` returns a canonical person's accepted claims grouped by Source and ordered by Source title, each claim with its Evidence graph, Subject and that Subject's Observations (a Subject-valued Observation carries the other Subject's label), served from the graph cache with no query when warm. The app reads it under a new `.personClaims` key. One `Node.AcceptedMembers` helper replaces the private count, `conclusionheaders.SortByTitle` is exported, and a loaded Evidence graph carries its ref, name and its Source's ref and type (a Source type change now drops it). A key now declares the write effects it follows in its descriptor's `refreshes` list, and `apply` revalidates each key once; the Evidence graph, the Persons, Events and Places lists and the detail page moved onto it from hand-written fan-outs, and `.personClaims` follows its own handle and the graphs it holds a claim on. No view reads it until the Sources section (S13-16).

## S13-11 — Provenance sheet

**Landed 2026-10-31** in [#650](https://github.com/mendahu/provenencia/pull/650). Every Vitals row with records ends with a provenance control that opens a sheet: the shown value and its state, a summary, and each record's citation line (Source, Artifact, Citation, page), outcome and refs, with links to its Source, Citation and Observation. Records are grouped under their owning Event or Place (the shown one marked and spoken "shown value") and, for a mixed value, under the value they count under, with the rest last. The sheet follows its row across a refresh and closes when the row goes. The Person page no longer draws the Why; the Event and Place pages keep it, on the same `ReconciliationRecordRows`. `GetConclusionDetail`'s outcomes gain the Artifact label, Citation label, page and `group_rank` (Go groups the records; `locator.Page` reads the page). The sheet lists every kept value of a multi-valued row in its header, its summary reads "from 1 Source" for a single-Source value, a page above the 32-bit range shows no page, and each record is one VoiceOver element with its links as separate controls. A record's citation line is no longer selectable text; a copy button beside it copies it, through the kit's new `PVClipboardButton` (copy glyph, then a green check for 1.6 seconds, announced to VoiceOver). The birth and death place rows list the records of the event–place link (the register's note that the event took place there), each read as its Place's toponym, grouped under the Place, one group per Place however many links reach it (a Place alone has no group line, and the summary counts Places). A link's record reads as its Place's first toponym by one rule shared with Event titles.

## S13-25 — The pin table goes

**Landed 2026-10-30** in [#644](https://github.com/mendahu/provenencia/pull/644). An Identity Claim is membership only: subject, entity, status. One format step (`000058.sql`) drops the Observation pin table, the claim's confidence grade and argument columns, and the `claim_confidence_grades` table, keeping every claim and every old audit row. The pin delete facet and its confirm line, `identityclaims` pins, the effects entries, the grades package and its create-time seed, and the Mac app's "removed from the evidence for" sentence are deleted. The data-model, delete and vocabulary docs say the table and columns were dropped.

## S13-06 — Derived rows carry their records

**Landed 2026-10-30** in [#640](https://github.com/mendahu/provenencia/pull/640). The Person page's birth date, birth place, death date and death place rows now show a Why like every other Vitals row. `GetConclusionDetail` on a Person returns `derived_rows`: for each life row, the reconciler records of the field it reads, keyed by the Event or Place that owns it (every surviving birth or death event's date, lead first; each of those events' Places' names once). With several owners the Why groups the records under a line titled as the Events and Places lists title them, the shown owner's in primary ink and the rest secondary; the place row's open arrow now opens the shown Place. `fieldsOf` composes any number of handles' fields in constant queries, and `ForEntity` is its one-handle caller.

## S13-24 — The pin engine goes

## S13-23 — Promote stops asking for pins

**Landed 2026-10-29** in [#633](https://github.com/mendahu/provenencia/pull/633). Promote is a quick action: a researcher picks a target per row and presses Done. The evidence sheet, the assessment column (band, Contested mark, reason, chevron) and the confidence grade read are gone; the rows, headings and Already promoted rows share one grid (`PromoteGridRow`), and the list widens to 1600 pt with a 460 pt target menu at Wide. The share wording, five wire enums and their parity rows go, and every Promote string says promote, never file.
