Most recent first, so the first row is what one click of Back does. Forward mirrors it, ordered outward from the current view.
Row anatomy Three columns on a 15px / fluid / auto grid. The destination glyph is the same one the sidebar uses, so a row is recognisable before it is read. Then the trail: the root destination in caption-size muted Spectral, a faint ›, and the leaf in semibold body. A destination-level entry drops the root and prints only the destination name — no Sources › Sources. The trailing reference is mono and faint: it identifies the record without competing with its title. Rows truncate the leaf with an ellipsis; the reference never truncates.
How the menu is reached
Click One step. No menu. Press and hold 400ms on the button opens the menu — the Safari and Finder gesture, and the one people find by accident. The click that ends the hold is swallowed, so holding never also navigates. Right-click / ⌃-click Opens the menu immediately. Same content as the hold. ⌘[ and ⌘] Back and forward by one, from anywhere in the window. No disclosure arrow A visible caret on a 26px control reads as a second button and is not how the platform draws this. The affordance is the gesture, stated in the tooltip.
Rules Disabled at either end of the stack — never a menu with no rows. Dismiss on outside click, Escape, or selection; selecting a row moves the pointer in the stack rather than truncating it, so Forward survives a jump backwards. A new destination visited from a mid-stack position drops everything ahead of it, as a browser does. The stack holds the last 12 views per window and is not persisted across launches.
Deliberately absent No source rows, ingest, or vocabulary editors (S2-02 – S2-04). No disabled nav items for Interpretation, People, or Settings. No evidence grades, credibility badges, or lineage colors in chrome. No project created / updated / updated-by bookkeeping. No tabs, multi-window model, or second content panel.
